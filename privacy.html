Privacy Policy

This extension modifies the layout of YouTube watch pages so the video player stays fixed while the recommendations sidebar remains scrollable.

What data we collect

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. It does not track browsing activity, search history, watch history, or user accounts.

It does not use analytics, advertising IDs, cookies, or remote servers. It also does not access or send your login data, payment information, or account data.

What the extension accesses

The extension reads the structure and geometry of the current YouTube page only to determine where the video player and sidebar are located and apply the sticky layout behavior in the browser.

This information is used only on your device for the extension's purpose. It is never shared with third parties.

Third-party services

This extension does not use third-party analytics, data brokers, tracking tools, or external services.

Changes to this policy

If the extension changes in a way that affects data handling, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the extension listing or project repository.
